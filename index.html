See what's new on the shelf at the store you're standing in.

The gap

Hundreds of better-for-you products launch every year, but shoppers rarely notice them. Retail shelves don't flag what's new, and searching online only helps if you already know what to look for.

What it does

  • Opens when you walk into a store and shows that store's new launches by aisle
  • Puts products that fit your goals first, including high protein, low sugar and plant-based
  • Gives brands a paid way to get seen in their first 90 days on shelf

Who it's for

Health-conscious shoppers who care about flavor and fun branding, and the emerging brands trying to reach them.

About this demo

You pick the store here. The live version would open the right store when you walk in, or when you scan a code on the shelf. Links like #target or #sprouts-l587 simulate both.

Shows only better-for-you products that are brand-new in 2026 (not existing items newly stocked). They come from launches announced in 2026 press releases (BevNET, NOSH) and trade coverage (Snack Food & Wholesale Bakery, Refrigerated & Frozen Foods, Beverage Industry, Prepared Foods). Availability is chain-wide, so a given store may not stock every item yet.

The Featured slot shows what a brand's paid placement looks like to shoppers.

Iteration

How it got here

  1. Defined the gap

    New better-for-you products are hard to discover in store. Google only helps if you already know what to search for. The bet is a guide that knows which store you're in.

  2. 40-item prototype

    Parallel research agents hand-curated 40 2026 launches across snacks, beverages, and pantry, fridge and freezer. Built as mobile web for Sprouts and Target first.

  3. Shopper features

    Aisle grouping, health filters, "Only here" and "Limited" badges, a detail sheet with source links, and Save for next trip.

  4. Brand portal

    Added the paying side, including engagement metrics, placement tiers (Listed, Featured, Spotlight) and a launch submission form.

  5. Real product photos

    Sourced from brand catalogs, retailer pages and press images, packed into sprite sheets so the page stays one fast file.

  6. Scaled to 725 launches

    Agents read about 3,500 BevNET and NOSH press releases and about 1,500 trade-magazine articles and extracted structured records.

  7. Cut to 320

    Kept only items that are better-for-you and genuinely new in 2026, not existing products newly stocked somewhere.

  8. Nutrition facts

    Looked up label data for each product, shown as a real Nutrition Facts panel with its source. Medium-confidence items are flagged.

  9. Visual polish

    Shelf-tag styling with outlines and offset shadows, a sticker-style title, and fixes found while testing at phone width.

  10. The in-store moment

    Entry screen when you walk in, shelf-tag QR links, goals on entry, "Just landed" recency, "Spotted it here" shopper confirmations, and a projector-friendly frame.

Stack

What it's built with

Builder
Claude, working in a Claude Project that holds the datasets
Research
Parallel Claude subagents for sourcing, extraction and nutrition lookups
Data
Python to clean, dedupe, filter and package the records as JSON and CSV
Front end
One HTML file with plain JavaScript and CSS, no framework
Images
Product photos packed into five sprite sheets
Testing
Headless-browser screenshots at phone width after each change
Hosting
Published as a Claude artifact, viewable on any phone browser
